Who we are

Vice Cutroom is an independent fan page that shares edited GTA 6 clips. Contact: vicecutroom40@gmail.com.

What Vice Cutroom Poster collects

When the Vice Cutroom account signs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and refresh token, the account's TikTok open ID, display name and username, and the list of privacy options TikTok allows for that account. Nothing else about the account or its followers is requested.

How it is used

These details are used only to send Vice Cutroom's own videos to the signed-in TikTok account. They are stored on the computer that runs the tool, in a file readable only by its owner. They are not uploaded anywhere else.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account stays connected. To disconnect, remove the app in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites, or email vicecutroom40@gmail.com and the stored tokens will be deleted.

Children

The tool is not directed to children and is not offered to the public.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
